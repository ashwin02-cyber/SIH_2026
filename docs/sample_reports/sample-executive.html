Recommended actions

PriorityAction
HighThe key-exchange group, Group 2 (1024-bit MODP), is too small for modern threats. Switch to DH group 14 at minimum, preferably an elliptic-curve group (19, 20 or 21).
MediumPrefer an AES-GCM cipher over AES-CBC-128: it protects integrity as well as confidentiality and needs no separate HMAC.
MediumConfirm in the VPN gateway configuration that Perfect Forward Secrecy is enabled. It cannot be seen in a passive capture.
LowTransport mode leaves the original IP header visible. Use tunnel mode for gateway-to-gateway links unless transport mode is required.
MediumRepeat the analysis with a capture that includes the IKE negotiation (start the capture before the tunnel comes up), so the settings are read from the traffic itself instead of from the file name.

What this report cannot tell you

  • An IPsec capture only shows the cipher and key-exchange group when the start of the VPN negotiation was captured. Whether forward secrecy is on, and tunnel versus transport mode, are negotiated in an encrypted step and are not visible to an observer.
  • The traffic-type label is a statistical guess from packet sizes and timing, trained on five scripted traffic types. It is not proof of what users did.
